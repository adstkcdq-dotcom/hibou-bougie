## Full description
Hibou & Bougie ("Owl & Candle") is a focus timer you play like a game. Start a session, put your phone down and focus: meanwhile, your owl's library grows all by itself. When the timer ends, you earn stars.

Perfect for studying, homework, exam revision, reading, work or your own projects. It is a calm, cosy idle game that rewards your focus instead of distracting you — and helps you stop procrastinating.

🕯️ A TIMER TO HELP YOU FOCUS
• Pick your duration, from 10 minutes to 3 hours: a short pomodoro (25 min) or a long study session
• Choose your activity: study, work, reading, sport, creative…
• During the timer, your library makes 3 times more, even with your phone locked
• A notification tells you when the session is over
• No ads during the timer

🦉 AN OWL THAT GROWS WITH YOU
• Your owl grows with your focus hours: owlet, young student, scholar, then great sage
• It sleeps while you work and wakes up happy at the end
• Dress it up: glasses, scarf, headphones, quill… five tiers, from wood to crystal
• Change the colour of its feathers

📚 A LIVING LIBRARY
• 12 objects to collect: candles, tea, lamp, bookshelves, librarian cat, telescope, globe, hourglass, fireflies, flying grimoires, orrery and the portal of knowledge
• The window follows the real time of day: sunrise, daytime, sunset and starry night
• Grow the room from a reading nook to a celestial library

📊 YOUR PROGRESS
• Stats: total time, last 7 days, day streak, time per activity
• 19 achievements to unlock
• A collection of 24 books, including rare and legendary ones, earned with every session

✨ SIMPLE, CALM, NO PRESSURE
• A short tutorial guides you on first launch
• Ads are optional: you choose to watch one to get a bonus
• No account needed, your game stays on your phone
• Works offline
• In English and French

Light the candle — the owl is waiting for you.
